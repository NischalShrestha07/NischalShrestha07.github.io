About

Hi, I'm Narayan.

Computer Engineering student and backend developer from Biratnagar, Nepal. I like the unglamorous half of a product: the schema, the permissions, the API contract — the parts that decide whether everything above them stays standing.

I started with PHP, fell into Laravel, and stayed. Since then I've built gym management, payments, HR, CRM and ticketing systems — most of them multi-tenant, all of them behind a role-based permission layer.

My interest is in systems that survive their second year: sane migrations, boring abstractions, tests where they matter, and an API someone else can read without asking me first. I'm still a student, and I still ship.
